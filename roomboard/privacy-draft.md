# RoomBoard Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 29, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 29, 2026

---

## Introduction

RoomBoard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application RoomBoard (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When you create an account and use RoomBoard, we collect:

| Data Type | Purpose | How Collected |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| **Display Name** | To identify you within your household | From your Apple ID or Google account at sign-in |
| **Email Address** | Account identification and recovery | From your Apple ID or Google account at sign-in |
| **User ID** | To uniquely identify your account | Generated by Firebase Authentication |
| **Household Membership** | To associate you with your households | When you create or join a household |
| **Chore Completion Data** | To calculate fairness scores and track history | When you complete tasks in the App |

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect your location
- We do **not** access your photos, camera, or microphone
- We do **not** access your contacts or calendar
- We do **not** collect device identifiers for advertising
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Provide the Service** — Create and manage your account, associate you with households, assign chores, and track completions.

2. **Calculate Fairness** — Our fairness algorithm uses your completion history to equitably distribute tasks among household members.

3. **Sync Across Devices** — Your data is stored in Firebase Firestore so you can access it from any device signed into your account.

4. **Authenticate Your Identity** — We use Firebase Authentication with Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In to securely verify your identity.

We do **not** use your information for:
- Advertising or marketing
- Selling to third parties
- Profiling or behavioral targeting
- Analytics beyond basic app functionality

---

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

- **Firebase Authentication** — Manages your sign-in credentials using industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocols.
- **Firebase Firestore** — Stores your household data, chore assignments, and completion history in Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit.

We implement security rules to ensure you can only access:
- Your own user profile
- Households you are a member of
- Chore data within those households

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

We share data only:

1. **With Your Household Members** — Other members of your household can see your display name, assigned chores, and completion status. This is the core functionality of the App.

2. **With Service Providers** — We use Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Firebase processes data on our behalf under their privacy policies:
   - [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
   - [Google Cloud Privacy](https://cloud.google.com/security/privacy)

3. **As Required by Law** — We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

---

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access Your Data
You can view all your data within the App in the Settings screen.

### Delete Your Account
You can delete your account at any time from **Settings > Delete Account**. This will:
- Permanently delete your user profile
- Remove your membership from all households
- Delete your chore completion history

Account deletion is irreversible. Data may take up to 30 days to be fully purged from backup systems.

### Sign Out
You can sign out at any time from Settings. This removes your local session but preserves your account for future sign-in.

### Household Data
If you leave a household, your membership is removed but historical completion records may be retained for fairness calculations (without identifying information).

---

## Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Account Profile** | Until you delete your account |
| **Household Membership** | Until you leave the household or delete your account |
| **Chore Completions** | Rolling history retained for fairness calculations |

After you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days from all active systems.

How to Use

Simple for households, practical for daily life. Get started in minutes.

Create a Household

Set up your household and invite the people who share chores, responsibilities, or common spaces.

Add Tasks

Record recurring or one-time chores so everyone can see what needs attention next.

Track Completions

Mark tasks done as you go and keep a clear history of who completed what.

Balance Fairly

Use completion history to make sure chores are distributed more evenly over time.

Use Anywhere

Stay in Sync

Everyone sees the same household state, helping your family stay coordinated and informed.
